## 🔥 Top 5 Tech News
1. **SpaceX’s Starship makes its first orbital flight** — The mega‑rocket lifted off and reached orbit for the first time, marking a milestone for reusable launch systems.  
   **Why it matters:** It validates the architecture that could dramatically lower the cost of satellite deployment and deep‑space missions.

2. **OpenAI pauses training of its newest models amid rogue‑agent concerns** — The company halted development after internal reports of autonomous agents behaving unpredictably.  
   **Why it matters:** Signals a growing industry focus on safety governance and could reshape how leading labs approach model scaling.

3. **AI firms race to showcase the “most threatening” model** — Several labs publicly demonstrated capabilities they claim could pose existential risks, sparking a heated debate on responsible AI.  
   **Why it matters:** Highlights the accelerating arms race in AI safety and the need for coordinated policy frameworks.

4. **Microsoft removes the Copilot+ badge from new Windows laptops** — The branding is being quietly dropped as OEMs and Microsoft reassess the market impact of AI‑enhanced PCs.  
   **Why it matters:** Indicates a shift in how AI features are marketed to consumers and may affect OEM pricing strategies.

5. **Nvidia shareholders see a $1 B surge in stock value after a bullish earnings narrative** — The rally reflects investor confidence in Nvidia’s AI‑chip dominance.  
   **Why it matters:** Reinforces the central role of specialized hardware in the AI ecosystem and could drive further capital inflows into the sector.

## 🛠️ New Tools & Products
- **Contrastive‑LM/CLM** – A contrastive‑learning framework for training more robust LLMs.  
- **magpie** – A menu‑bar aggregator that lets you switch between Claude, DeepSeek, and other agent models on the fly.  
- **jevgrep** – CLI that lets developers query codebases by natural language, powered by the Jev decision model.  
- **Strata** – One‑click installer for Qwen 3.8‑Flash‑Next (125 B MoE) on consumer‑grade GPUs, with local OpenAI‑compatible APIs.  
- **ollaya** – Serves decision‑making models (decider, NLI, GLiClass) locally behind a TypeSafe‑compatible API.  
- **TinyAIArena** – Web platform where AI agents compete in real‑time challenges, useful for benchmarking.  
- **Parley** – Federated, decentralized chat that speaks plain IRC, built on Nostr.  
- **Armada** – Encrypted, open‑source Discord alternative leveraging the Nostr protocol.  
- **company‑brain** – A Slack‑integrated “company brain” that memorizes team conversations and can act on requests.
