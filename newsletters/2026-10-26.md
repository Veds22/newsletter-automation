# 🗞️ AI & Tech Weekly — 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-26

## 🔥 Top 5 Tech News
1. **Anthropic files S‑1 to go public** — The AI‑focused research lab has filed a registration statement to list on a U.S. exchange, marking the first major AI IPO since 2023.  
   **Why it matters:** It signals confidence in the commercial viability of large‑scale foundation models and could set a valuation benchmark for other AI startups.

2. **OpenAI and Anthropic slash LLM prices by up to 80 %** — Both companies announced steep price cuts for their flagship models, citing economies of scale and more efficient hardware.  
   **Why it matters:** The move intensifies the “price war” in the LLM market, making powerful models accessible to startups and could pressure smaller providers to consolidate or specialize.

3. **IBM CEO says open‑weight models are now good and cheap on‑prem** — IBM’s chief executive claims that open‑weight, locally‑hosted models now match the performance of proprietary offerings at a fraction of the cost.  
   **Why it matters:** This could accelerate on‑prem AI adoption in regulated industries (finance, healthcare) and reduce the cloud‑centric monopoly of big AI vendors.

4. **Microsoft explains launch of the 9B‑parameter Qwen‑3.5 model** — A detailed post outlines the technical and strategic rationale behind releasing a compact, high‑quality LLM aimed at edge and low‑budget workloads.  
   **Why it matters:** Democratizing strong LLMs at a 9‑billion‑parameter scale lowers the barrier for developers to embed sophisticated language capabilities in products without massive infrastructure.

5. **Tesla cancels Cybertruck rollout in Australia and New Zealand** — After seven years of deposits, Tesla announced it will not ship the futuristic pickup to these markets.  
   **Why it matters:** Highlights the challenges of scaling novel vehicle platforms globally and may signal a shift in Tesla’s focus toward more profitable, higher‑volume models.

## 🧠 New AI/Tech Research

## 🛠️ New Tools & Products
- **Flare Local Inference** — NVIDIA’s open‑source library for running large models on a single GPU with minimal latency.  
- **3D‑model‑anything** — Capture iPhone LiDAR data and generate editable Three.js scenes directly in the browser.  
- **gpu‑unlock‑ada** — Community‑maintained firmware that unlocks tensor cores on RTX 4090 (and experimental RTX 4070 Ti SUPER) for broader AI workloads.  
- **PaperPilot** — Pure‑Rust, zero‑cloud PDF engine with WebAssembly support, enabling fast, privacy‑preserving document processing.  
- **Aerial TV** — Ad‑free, tvOS‑style Android TV launcher for Chinese‑market smart TVs, offering a clean desktop experience.
