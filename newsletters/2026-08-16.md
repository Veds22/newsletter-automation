# 🗞️ AI & Tech Weekly — 2026-08-09 to 2026-08-16

## 🔥 Top 5 Tech News
1. **Anthropic releases “Patterns and problems in emerging multi‑agent systems”** — The paper maps coordination bottlenecks, scaling limits, and safety gaps as LLM agents become more autonomous.  
   **Why it matters:** It sets the research agenda for the next generation of AI agents and will shape product road‑maps across the industry.  

2. **Claude adds System Prompts** — Developers can now define a reusable “system prompt” that presets Claude’s behavior across sessions.  
   **Why it matters:** Consistent persona and policy enforcement become trivial, lowering the barrier for building reliable AI assistants.  

3. **Firefox for iOS ships a native ad‑blocker** — Mozilla’s iOS client now includes built‑in ad‑blocking without extensions.  
   **Why it matters:** Strengthens privacy on a major mobile browser and pressures ad networks to adapt to stricter user controls.  

4. **ShieldBreak: a Windows Defender 0‑day disclosed** — Security researcher MSNightmare published an exploit that bypasses core Windows Defender components.  
   **Why it matters:** A critical flaw in a default Windows security layer forces urgent patches and highlights supply‑chain attack vectors.  

5. **“AI Credit Resale Economy” report uncovers token brokers** — Vectoral details a growing market where LLM‑generated content is tokenised and resold.  
   **Why it matters:** Raises regulatory, security, and ethical questions about the commodification of AI output and potential abuse.
