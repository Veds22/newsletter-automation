# 🗞️ AI & Tech Weekly — 2026-08-09 to 2026-08-16

## 🔥 Top 5 Tech News
1. **Claude adds System Prompts** — Claude now lets developers supply a system‑level prompt that shapes model behavior across all conversations.  
   *Why it matters:* Fine‑grained control improves safety, alignment, and product differentiation for teams building on Claude.

2. **Anthropic publishes “Patterns and problems in emerging multi‑agent systems”** — The paper maps scalability bottlenecks, coordination failures, and design patterns for LLM‑driven agents.  
   *Why it matters:* Multi‑agent coordination is the next frontier for AI assistants; understanding its pitfalls will steer research and product roadmaps.

3. **Vectoral exposes the AI credit resale economy** — A deep dive reveals “token brokers” who buy, split, and resell LLM usage credits, creating a shadow market.  
   *Why it matters:* Unregulated credit trading could enable abuse of expensive AI models and raise new security‑compliance challenges.

4. **Firefox for iOS ships a native ad‑blocker** — The mobile browser now blocks ads at the OS level without extensions.  
   *Why it matters:* Sets a new privacy baseline for mobile browsing and pressures competing browsers to adopt similar built‑in protections.

5. **DeepSeek launches DeepSeek Harness (DSH)** — An open‑source plugin framework that turns any LLM into a modular platform; the repo hit 129 k stars in days.  
   *Why it matters:* Signals a shift toward extensible LLM ecosystems, lowering the barrier for developers to add custom tools, data sources, and UI skins.
