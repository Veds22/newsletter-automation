# 🗞️ AI & Tech Weekly — 2026-08-17 to 2026-08-24

## 🔥 Top 5 Tech News
1. **Iranian hackers shut down a UK power plant for four days** — State‑backed actors breached the plant’s control systems, forcing a prolonged outage and raising alarms about the vulnerability of critical energy infrastructure.  
   **Why it matters:** It underscores the growing geopolitical use of cyber‑weapons against essential services and will likely drive stricter regulatory scrutiny and investment in industrial‑control security.

2. **Malware infects Android‑based automotive head‑unit firmware** — Researchers uncovered a new trojan that can persist on infotainment systems, giving attackers remote code execution and data exfiltration capabilities.  
   **Why it matters:** As cars become software‑defined, supply‑chain attacks on vehicle firmware could become a major vector for large‑scale espionage or ransomware.

3. **OpenAI cuts GPT‑5.6 Sol pricing by 20%** — The latest “Sol” variant of GPT‑5.6 sees a significant price reduction, making high‑throughput inference cheaper for enterprises.  
   **Why it matters:** Pricing pressure may accelerate adoption of large‑scale LLMs and intensify competition among AI providers to offer cost‑effective compute.

4. **Wi‑Fi 8 is the first wireless upgrade in years that isn’t chasing speed** – The Wi‑Fi Alliance announced the Wi‑Fi 8 standard, focusing on latency, reliability, and AI‑assisted spectrum management rather than raw bandwidth.  
   **Why it matters:** It signals a shift toward network intelligence and could reshape home/enterprise networking, especially for edge AI workloads.

5. **Anthropic appears to be A/B testing reduced effort levels in Claude Code** – Internal observations suggest Anthropic is throttling the “effort” parameter for Claude Code, potentially limiting token usage per request.  
   **Why it matters:** If true, it may indicate a move to control compute costs or to experiment with tiered performance, affecting developer productivity and pricing models.
